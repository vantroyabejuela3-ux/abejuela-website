About Sweet Crumbs

Sweet Crumbs started with one oven and a stack of family recipes. Today it bakes birthday cakes, cupcake boxes, and weekly pastry specials for neighbors, students, and offices.

Everything is baked in small batches after you order, so please book early for events.
